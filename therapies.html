Our Therapies

Care for your wellbeing

Doctor-led Ayurvedic care, yoga and rehabilitation. Online appointments are available for consultations; all hands-on therapies are provided at the clinic.

Ayurvedic Consultation

A detailed review of your health concerns, history, diet, routine and Prakriti (individual constitution), followed by a personalised, clearly explained care plan.

Led by Dr. Raju Bhusanar & Dr. Anil Kumar Vaidhya

Panchakarma Therapy

A personalised programme of selected classical procedures, planned after consultation and carried out by trained staff under a doctor's supervision.

Led by Dr. Raju Bhusanar · See the five karmas

Detox Therapy

Doctor-guided 7, 14 or 21-day programmes combining selected therapies, diet guidance, herbal support and follow-up, based on individual suitability.

Led by Dr. Raju Bhusanar

Internal Medicine

Ayurvedic assessment and ongoing care for digestive, joint, skin, stress-related, metabolic and other long-term health concerns.

Led by Dr. Anil Kumar Vaidhya, MD (Kayachikitsa)

Yoga Therapy

Personalised Hatha Yoga, pranayama, meditation and Yoga Pilates sessions designed to support mobility, balance, breath awareness, relaxation and overall wellbeing.

Led by Yaashiika Y. Sawant · Om Advaita Yog Institute

Physiotherapy

Assessment-led rehabilitation focused on pain, mobility, posture, strength and recovery, coordinated with your wider care plan when appropriate.

Led by Dr. Nikhil & Dr. Sahil M.K.
